The story

History myths debunked, one by one. Did Vikings really wear horned helmets? Was Napoleon actually short? Did medieval people believe the Earth was flat? Can you really see the Great Wall of China from space with the naked eye? Did Marie Antoinette say "let them eat cake"? Were the Salem witches burned at the stake? Did Roman gladiators always fight to the death? Did Einstein fail math? This is the real history behind eight famous historical lies and common misconceptions, and the exact people who invented each one. A tour through history's most successful misinformation, from ancient Rome to the medieval world: who invented each myth, in which century, and why the real history is stranger than the lie.
