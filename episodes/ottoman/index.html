The Ottoman Empire Killed Rome — Then Killed Itself

23 July 2026 · 13 min watch · hand-drawn animated history

▶

The short answer

The Ottoman Empire began as a tiny band of frontier raiders and grew into the empire that finished the Roman story: in 1453 a twenty-one-year-old sultan broke the thousand-year walls of Constantinople and ended the last Roman Empire. For more than six centuries the Ottomans ruled across three continents, then dismantled themselves in barely twelve years. This is the complete rise and fall of the Ottoman Empire.

The story

The full history of the Ottoman Empire explained: how Osman founded the empire around the year 1300, how Mehmed the Conqueror took Constantinople in 1453, how the devshirme levy and the janissaries built the first professional army in Europe since Rome, the golden age of Suleiman the Magnificent, the turning point at the gates of Vienna in 1683, why the Ottomans became the 'sick man of Europe,' and how the empire finally collapsed through the Balkan Wars and the First World War. A Doodle History documentary on how the Ottoman Empire rose, ruled, and fell.
