Genghis Khan Started as a Boy Left for Dead

The short answer

A starving orphan his own clan abandoned to die on the freezing steppe became the founder of the largest land empire in history. This is the true story of Genghis Khan and the rise of the Mongol Empire — and the one idea that let him outgrow the world that threw him away.

The story

The Mongol Empire explained — how Genghis Khan rose from an abandoned, enslaved boy to forge the largest contiguous land empire in history, sweeping from China to the gates of Europe, and how the empire fractured almost as fast as it was built. A military history of the medieval world: Genghis Khan's armies, the sieges and conquests that built the largest contiguous land empire in history, and the trade routes it connected.
