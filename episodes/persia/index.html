The Persian Empire vs "300": How Much Was Actually True?

20 August 2026 · 13 min watch · hand-drawn animated history

▶

The short answer

The Persian Empire once ruled nearly half of humanity — then got flipped. This is the full history of the Achaemenid Persian Empire: how Cyrus the Great, a minor vassal, built the largest empire the world had ever seen, and how it was remembered as the villain by the Greeks who finally beat it.

The story

The Persian Empire explained, from Cyrus the Great to the fall of the Achaemenid dynasty. How did the Persian Empire rise and fall? We follow Cyrus, Cambyses, Darius the Great and Xerxes through the conquest of Babylon and Lydia, the Royal Road and the satrapies, the gold daric, and the Greco-Persian Wars at Marathon, Thermopylae and Salamis — up to Alexander the Great's conquest at Gaugamela and the burning of Persepolis. Was ancient Persia really history's cruelest empire, or just the empire whose enemies wrote the story? A military history of the ancient world: the Achaemenid armies, Marathon and Thermopylae, and the empire that stretched from Egypt to India.
