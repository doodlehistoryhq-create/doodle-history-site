Alexander the Great Died at 32. Why Did His Empire Die With Him?

27 August 2026 · 11 min watch · hand-drawn animated history

▶

The short answer

Alexander the Great built the largest empire the ancient world had yet seen — and it shattered within two years of his death. This is the history of the Macedonian Empire: its lightning rise under Alexander and its sudden, brutal collapse.

The story

How did Alexander the Great's empire fall apart so fast? From Philip the Second's Macedonian war machine and the eighteen-foot sarissa phalanx, through the battles of Granicus, Issus and the decisive clash at Gaugamela against Darius the Third of Persia, to the siege of Tyre, the mutiny at the Hyphasis in India, and the deathbed answer "to the strongest" — this is the full story of Alexander the Great explained: the conquest of the Persian Empire, his death at thirty-two in Babylon, and the Wars of the Diadochi that tore his empire apart into the Hellenistic successor kingdoms of Ptolemaic Egypt, the Seleucid Empire and Antigonid Macedon.
