The short answer

The Sumerian King List says its first king ruled for 28,800 years. So how much of Ancient Sumer's history is real? The sourced story of the first cities, the first writing, Queen Puabi's death pit, the fall of Ur, and why the Anunnaki were never aliens.

The story

Ancient Sumer explained: the history of Mesopotamia's first civilization, from Eridu and Uruk to the fall of Ur around 2004 BCE. Ancient history and archaeology from the Royal Cemetery of Ur (Sir Leonard Woolley, Queen Puabi, the Great Death Pit and the 2011 CT-scan study), Sargon of Akkad and Enheduanna, the world's first named author, Ur-Nammu's law code and the Great Ziggurat of Ur, the Lament for Ur, cuneiform writing and the Sumerian King List, the Epic of Gilgamesh flood tablet George Smith read in 1872, and Ea-nasir's complaint tablet. A myth-vs-reality look at the Anunnaki ancient-aliens claims against what the Sumerian tablets actually say.
