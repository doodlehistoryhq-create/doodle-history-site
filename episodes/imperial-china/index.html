Imperial China Died a Dozen Times and Refused to Stay Dead

The short answer

China is not the oldest unbroken empire on Earth — it is something far stranger. This is the rise and fall of imperial China: an empire that violently collapsed and was reborn a dozen times across two thousand years, from the First Emperor Qin Shi Huang to the last emperor Puyi, all driven by the Mandate of Heaven.

The story

Imperial China explained — the full sweep of the Chinese dynasties, from Qin Shi Huang and the Terracotta Army through the Han, Tang, Song, Ming and Qing, the Mandate of Heaven, and the dynastic cycle that let China collapse and be reborn a dozen times across two thousand years.
