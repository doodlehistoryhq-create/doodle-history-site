The Odyssey: How Much of It Was Actually Real?

25 July 2026 · 13 min watch · hand-drawn animated history

▶

The short answer

Homer's Odyssey is the most famous adventure ever told: a one-eyed giant, a witch who turns men into pigs, a ten-year voyage home. But how much of it was ever real? This is the history behind the myth, where the fairy tale ends and the real Bronze Age begins.

The story

The Odyssey explained, and the true story behind it. We follow Odysseus from Troy through the Cyclops, the Sirens, Scylla and Charybdis, Circe and Calypso to his homecoming in Ithaca, then ask the questions everyone is searching after Christopher Nolan's new film: Is the Odyssey a true story? Was the Trojan War real? Did Troy exist? Who was Homer? We look at Heinrich Schliemann's dig at Hisarlik, Mycenaean Greece and the Linear B tablets, the Late Bronze Age Collapse, and the boar's-tusk helmet that proves a sung poem carried a real memory across five hundred years. Part ancient history, part archaeology: Bronze Age Greece, the excavations at Troy, and how scholars separate Homer's myth from Mycenaean history.
