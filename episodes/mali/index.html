Mansa Musa: The Richest Man Ever Began as a Boy Who Couldn't Walk

13 September 2026 · 12 min watch · hand-drawn animated history

▶

The short answer

The Mali Empire made Mansa Musa, by most reckonings the richest human who ever lived — but it began with Sundiata Keita, a prince who, by legend, was born unable to walk. This is the true story of medieval West Africa's golden empire: the mocked, exiled boy who founded it, the trans-Saharan gold that made its king famous as far away as Europe, and how the whole thing quietly faded.

The story

The Mali Empire explained, from Sundiata Keita and the Battle of Kirina to Mansa Musa's staggering 1324 pilgrimage to Mecca. How did the trans-Saharan gold and salt trade make medieval Mali one of the richest empires in history? Why did Mansa Musa's gold crash the price of gold in Egypt? How did Timbuktu grow into a legendary center of Islamic learning — and how did the Mali Empire fall to the rising Songhai? The full history of medieval West Africa: Sundiata, Mansa Musa, Timbuktu, and the gold that helped fund the medieval world. A history of medieval African civilization: the armies and battles that built the empire, the trans-Saharan trade that funded it, and the manuscripts of Timbuktu that preserved its scholarship.
