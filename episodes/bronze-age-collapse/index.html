The Bronze Age Collapse: Who Killed Every Empire at Once?

17 September 2026 · 11 min watch · hand-drawn animated history

▶

The short answer

Three thousand years ago, nearly every superpower on Earth collapsed at almost the same moment — and no one has ever been able to say who, or what, killed them. This is the Bronze Age Collapse: the greatest cold case in ancient history.

The story

The Late Bronze Age Collapse explained: what caused the Bronze Age Collapse, who the Sea Peoples really were, and how a connected world of empires — Egypt, the Hittites, Mycenaean Greece, Ugarit and Babylon — all fell in roughly fifty years around 1200 BC. We walk the whole cascade one suspect at a time: a mega-drought, famine and the desperate grain letters, the earthquake storm, the Sea Peoples raids, the burning of Ugarit and Hattusa, systems collapse, and a Greek Dark Age so complete that people forgot how to write for three hundred years. This is ancient military and economic history in one cascade: invasions, famine, earthquakes and collapsing trade across the first connected civilization of the Mediterranean world.
